# Brownie pan: solution

Draw a straight line that goes through the center of the pan and the center of the
removed rectangle.

A line through the center of a rectangle divides it into two equal areas,
because the two sides are related by a half-turn around the center. So the line
divides both the original pan and the removed piece in half. Removing equal
areas from the two equal halves of the pan leaves equal amounts of brownie.
